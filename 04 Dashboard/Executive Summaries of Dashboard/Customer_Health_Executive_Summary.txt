Customer Health
The sheet provides an overview of customer engagement and support interactions, analyzing contact channels, customer segments, engagement recency, and complaint sentiment across 63,420 records.

Email is the most preferred contact channel with 22,983 records, followed by Web Portal (19,976) and Live Chat (14,132).

The Professional customer segment dominates with 30,839 records, accounting for nearly half of all interactions.

Days since last engagement peaks at 13 days with 5,249 records, suggesting a common re-engagement cycle.

Neutral sentiment drives the most complaints at 133, vastly outnumbering negative (26) and positive (8) complaints.
